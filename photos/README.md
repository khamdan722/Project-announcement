# Winner photographs

Three winners are individuals and need a portrait for the media team. The 2024
press release carried no images, so these are not placed in the generated
document — they are handed over alongside it unless you ask otherwise.

None of the documents uploaded under `winners so far/` contains a portrait:
Dr. Fakhro's CV has no images at all, the Al Madani letters carry only Dubai
Health letterhead, and Prof. Al-Shamsi's files hold a licence certificate and a
career timeline. Photographs have to be requested.

## What to request

A high-resolution portrait (300 dpi or better, at least 1500 px on the short
edge), with written permission to publish, from the winner or their institution's
communications office.

## Adding a photo once received

Save it here as `<winner-id>.jpg`, e.g. `arab-genetics-01.jpg`, and record the
filename and its permission in that winner's file under `photo` and
`photo_credit`.
